How it works

Your laptop becomes the house bank.

1

Run the installer

One command on any Linux machine. It sets up Node.js, copies the app and starts it every time the laptop boots.

2

Add your housemates

Each person gets a name and password. Free Tailscale gives you a private https address that works away from home.

3

Install it on your phones

Open the address and add it to your home screen. It opens like a normal app, with notifications on iPhone and Android.

Features

Everything a shared house argues about.

Built for real households with monthly bills, uneven splits and that one person who always forgets.

Add an expense like a text message

Type it the way you'd say it. The phone reads it on the spot, with no AI and no cost, and fills in the form for you to check. Try it:

Nudges, so you don't have to

Phone notifications when:

  • a bill is due tomorrow
  • someone adds an expense with you in it
  • it's your turn to take out the bins
  • you owe someone $5 or more on the 1st and 15th

Monthly bills

Rent, hydro and internet repeat on their own. Favourites add the usual pizza night with one tap and the same split.

Chores that rotate

Weekly, every two weeks or monthly, between the people you pick. Everyone sees whose turn it is.

Charts and a fair turn

See spending by month and category. "Last time it cost" flags when the hydro bill jumps. "Whose turn to pay" picks who's furthest behind.

Receipt photos and comments

Attach the receipt so nobody has to ask. Photos are shrunk on the phone and saved on your laptop, along with every comment, payment and settle-up.

Ask your AI assistant

A built-in MCP connector lets Muse, Claude or ChatGPT answer "what do I owe?" or "add groceries 54.20 to House". Your laptop just answers. The assistant does the thinking, so it costs you nothing.

Self-host

That old laptop in the closet is enough.

Split It is one Node.js file with no packages to install. All your data is a folder of plain JSON you can copy to a USB stick.

  • Linux (Ubuntu, Mint, Debian)
  • Node.js 22+
  • About 50 MB of disk
  • A free Tailscale account
  • Daily backups, last 30 kept automatically.
  • Starts on boot and keeps running with the lid closed.
  • Updating is the same command. Your data stays put.
~/split-it
# 1. Download and install
$ git clone https://github.com/sherrypanaich/split-it && cd split-it
$ bash install.sh

# 2. Add everyone who lives there
$ node server.js add-user Sam
$ node server.js add-user Alex

# 3. Reach it from anywhere, free
$ sudo tailscale funnel --bg 8080
  → https://old-laptop.tail1234.ts.net

    FAQ

    Questions housemates ask.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. The app is free and open source, Tailscale's free plan covers the web address, and quick add runs on the phone with no AI costs. The only running cost is the electricity for a laptop that's on all the time.

    Where does my data live?

    On your laptop, in a folder called data/. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Copy that folder somewhere safe every so often and you have a full backup, receipt photos included.

    Do my housemates need to install anything?

    No. They open the address in their phone browser and sign in. Adding it to the home screen makes it feel like a normal app and turns on notifications.

    What if the laptop is off?

    The app is down until it's back on. Nothing is lost, because everything is saved on the laptop. Set it to never sleep when plugged in and it'll just keep running.

    Can I run it on a Raspberry Pi, a Mac or a server?

    Anything that runs Node.js 22 or newer will work. The one-command installer is written for Debian-style Linux, so on other systems follow the manual steps in the README.
